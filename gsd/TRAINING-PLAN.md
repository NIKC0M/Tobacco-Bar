# GSD Puppy — 12-Week Foundation Plan

**Handler:** nicholas@roballen.co.za
**Puppy DOB:** ~2026-07-20 (age 7wk6d on 2026-09-13)
**Plan window:** 2026-09-13 → 2026-12-06 (age 8wk → ~20wk)
**Goals:** solid pet obedience · sport/working title foundation · fitness & conditioning

---

## The three non-negotiables

1. **Growth plates are open.** GSD plates close ~12–18 months. No jogging, no bike work,
   no repetitive stairs, no jumping down from heights, no slick floors, no forced
   distance. Damage here is permanent and it is the single most common way people
   wreck a working prospect before it ever competes.
2. **The socialisation window closes ~12–16 weeks.** Everything else on this list can be
   trained later. This cannot. It is the highest-priority item for the first 8 weeks
   of this plan.
3. **8–11 weeks is the first fear-imprint period.** One bad experience can set a
   lifelong pattern. Exposure must be *neutral and controlled*, never flooding.
   No dog parks. No forced greetings. Puppy chooses to approach, always.

## Goal reframe

| You asked for | At 8 weeks it actually means |
|---|---|
| Solid pet obedience | Marker/engagement foundation + management. Formal obedience is a byproduct, not the drill. |
| Sport / working title | Drive-building, grip development, engagement, puppy tracking. **Zero** formal heeling, no forced retrieve, no bitework. |
| Fitness & conditioning | Free play on varied surfaces + proprioception + staying lean. **No** structured cardio. |

## Block 1 — Weeks 1–4 (age 8–12wk): Socialisation blitz + foundations

**Priority: socialisation > everything.**

Socialisation targets (aim ~3–5 *quality* novel exposures/day, each ending neutral or positive):
- Surfaces: grass, gravel, tile, metal grate, wobbly board, sand, wet ground, stairs (carried up/down, 1–2 steps only for confidence)
- Sounds: traffic, vacuum, thunder/gunfire recordings at low volume paired with food, kitchen noise
- People: hats, beards, wheelchairs, umbrellas, children (supervised, brief), uniforms
- Handling: ears, paws, tail, mouth, collar grab, restraint on side — 30 seconds, paired with food
- Novelty: car rides (short, positive), vet-office visits with no procedure, crate in different rooms

Vaccination vs. parvo: follow AVSAB guidance — carry the pup in high-traffic areas, use
clean/known surfaces, only meet known vaccinated healthy dogs. Do not sit out the window.

Training (3–5 min sessions, 4–6x/day, always end while the pup still wants more):
- Charge the marker ("yes" or clicker) — 20 reps, then use it for everything
- Name response → food, 100% of the time, never used to scold
- Food/toy engagement: puppy offers eye contact → mark → pay
- Crate games: fed in crate, door closed for seconds, released before whining
- Potty protocol: out every 45–60 min, after sleep/play/food, mark + pay *outside*
- Bite inhibition: redirect to tug/chew, no yelping games, no hand-wrestling
- Tug mechanics: low, side-to-side (never vertical/airborne), let pup win often, no tooth-on-hand corrections

Sport foundation:
- **Start puppy tracking now** — 8–12wk is the ideal window. 5–10 short footsteps in
  grass, food in every step, pup on a line, nose down. 2–3x/week. This is free, safe,
  and builds a skill that is genuinely hard to install later.
- Build toy drive: rag/puppy tug, chase and prey movement, always end with pup possessing the toy

Conditioning:
- Free play only. Off-leash pottering on varied terrain = the real conditioning.
- Structured lead walks: ~5 min per month of age, max 2x/day (so ~10–15 min at this stage).
- Body condition: ribs easily felt, visible waist. **Keep the pup lean** — the single
  biggest controllable factor for hip/elbow outcomes.
- Large-breed puppy food with controlled calcium. **Do not supplement calcium.**

## Block 2 — Weeks 5–8 (age 12–16wk): Proofing + teething

- Socialisation continues but shifts to *quality and neutrality* — pup should be able to
  ignore a dog/person, not greet every one. This prevents adolescent reactivity/frustration.
- Teething (~16–24wk): expect regression in grip and tug. **Do not correct it.** Soften
  tugs, use softer material, keep sessions short. Grip problems are made here.
- Add: sit/down/place on a marker, recall games (two-person restrained recalls), hand target,
  loose-leash walking as a *game* (pressure-off = reward), settle on a mat
- Recall: build it on a long line only. Never call the pup to end fun. Never call if you
  can't enforce it.
- Begin brief separation conditioning — crate + absence, seconds → minutes. GSDs are
  velcro dogs and separation anxiety is preventable now, expensive later.
- Conditioning: add cavaletti (poles flat on ground, walk-over only), balance on a cushion,
  backing up, figure-8 around legs. Proprioception, not cardio.

## Block 3 — Weeks 9–12 (age 16–20wk): Early adolescence

- Expect the first "he's forgotten everything" phase. It is normal. Lower criteria, raise
  reinforcement rate, do not escalate corrections.
- Proof the basics in 3 new locations per week, low distraction first
- Recall under mild distraction, still on long line, for the next ~year
- Second fear period can appear ~6–14 months — flag it at check-in if confidence drops
- Sport: if IGP is the target, make contact with a club now and go *watch*. Pup should
  visit the field, see the environment, and leave before fatigue. No pressure work.
- Conditioning: walks now ~20–25 min structured, still no running. Hill *walking* is fine.
  Swimming (calm, supported) is excellent and joint-free if available.
- Book hip/elbow-aware vet chat: discuss neuter timing. **Delaying neuter past growth-plate
  closure (18–24mo) is associated with lower joint-disorder risk in GSDs** — worth an
  explicit conversation rather than a default 6-month desex.

## Daily template (all blocks)

| Slot | Content | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Potty → 1 training session → breakfast from hand/training | 10 min |
| Mid-morning | Socialisation outing (novel place, carried or short lead) | 15–20 min |
| Midday | Crate/settle + chew | — |
| Afternoon | Tracking or drive/tug session | 5–10 min |
| Evening | Free play on varied terrain + handling/grooming | 15 min |
| Night | Crate routine, last potty | — |

Sleep: a puppy this age needs **18–20 hours/day**. Most "bad behaviour" at 8–16 weeks is
overtiredness. Enforce naps in the crate.
